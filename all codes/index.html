    🎯 Practical Aim:
    To study the dependence of potential difference (V) across a resistor on the electric current (I) passing through it, determine its resistance, and verify Ohm's Law.
    📖 Scientific Principle & Ohm's Law Statement:
    "At constant temperature and physical state, the electric current (I) flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across its ends."
    V ∝ I  ⟹  V / I = R (Constant Resistance)  ⟹  V = I × R
    Where R is the resistance of the conductor in Ohms (Ω). For this lab practical, standard R = 10.00 Ω.

    🧰 Required Apparatus:

    🔋 DC Power Supply (6V) Provides continuous potential difference to drive current.
    🔑 Plug Key Switch used to safely close or open the circuit.
    🎚️ Rheostat (20 Ω) Variable resistor to adjust circuit resistance and current.
    ⚡ DC Ammeter (0–1 A) Measures circuit current (I). Always connected in series.
    📦 Standard Resistor (10 Ω) The component across which Ohm's Law is verified.
    🔋 DC Voltmeter (0–10 V) Measures potential difference (V). Connected in parallel.
    🔌 Connecting Wires Low resistance copper leads joining apparatus terminals.
