### 6. Feature 2 — Hints

Create a dedicated Hints option.

This must use the existing Progressive Hint Service.

Implement three levels:

Level 1 — Conceptual Hint:
Give a small conceptual clue without revealing the answer.

Level 2 — Directional Hint:
Guide the student towards the correct apparatus, connection or action.

Level 3 — Actionable Hint:
Give more specific guidance about how to correct the issue.

Example:

Student is connecting the ammeter incorrectly.

- Level 1: Think about how current flows through the circuit.
- Level 2: Check whether the ammeter should measure the current through the resistor.
- Level 3: Connect the ammeter in series with the resistor.

Requirements:
- Show one hint at a time.
- Add a "Show Next Hint" button.
- Add a "Back" and "Close" button.
- Avoid immediately revealing the complete answer.
- Use real simulation mistakes and progress when available.

- Current experiment stage.
- Next instruction.
